ABOUT ME

Maria Karla L. Gonzales is a resident of Ilaya, Calapan City, and was born in San Teodoro, Oriental Mindoro, on October 10, 1988. She is an alumna of the Bachelor of Fine Arts and Design program, majoring in Painting.

Her artistic inspiration is derived from nature, which she expresses through her floral and landscape paintings. Initially, she was inclined toward oil painting as her primary medium due to its technical qualities, but later shifted to acrylic for practical reasons. She is also skilled in digital media. Her use of color and some aspects of her subject matter are inspired by the works of Tom Wesselmann and Frida Kahlo.

MAJOR EXHIBITIONS: ORTIGAS ART FESTIVAL

Curated Exhibition Entry
Ortigas Art Festival (OAF) Exhibition

Title: Light tower Ilocos

Size: 18 inches x 24 inches

Medium: Acrylic on canvas

Subject: Cape Bojeador Lighthouse, Burgos, Ilocos Norte

Exhibited: Ortigas Art Festival

A commanding architectural and landscape scenery capturing Cape Bojeador Lighthouse, established during the Spanish colonial era. Built atop the scenic Vigia de Nagpartian Hill, the lighthouse continues to guide maritime travelers along the West Philippine Sea.

Curatorial Honor: Aman Sinaya

In connection with her festival showcase, artist Maria Karla dedicated a special curatorial tribute to Aman Sinaya — the primordial Southern Tagalog deity of the ocean and protectress of fishermen, celebrating Filipino coastal life.
